Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, do the same job. This page lists what Fixify’s site uses.

How we use them

The site works without analytics. We switch analytics on only if you choose “Accept” in the banner or turn on “Analytics” under “Preferences”. Until then, Google Tag Manager isn’t loaded and Google’s consent settings stay denied. We run no advertising on this site, so advertising storage stays denied.

Change or withdraw your choice

Use here or in the footer of any page. If you withdraw consent, we stop analytics and remove the Google Analytics cookies we can reach. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
