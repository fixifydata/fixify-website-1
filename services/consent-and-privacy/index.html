Questions.

Is my banner actually blocking tags?

Often not. We test what fires before and after each choice.

Are you a law firm?

No. We implement technical measurement. Please confirm legal requirements with your own counsel.

What is the difference between Basic and Advanced Consent Mode?

Basic blocks Google tags until consent. Advanced lets them send limited, cookieless signals. We explain which suits you.

Will consent hurt my data?

Some loss is normal. A good set-up keeps as much as the rules allow.
