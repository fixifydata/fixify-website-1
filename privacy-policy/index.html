This policy explains what personal data Fixify collects through this website, why, and what your rights are. We’ve tried to keep it plain.

Who we are

Fixify is an analytics and tracking consultancy. The organisation responsible for your data (the “controller”) is [LEGAL ENTITY NAME, REGISTRATION NUMBER], [LOCATION]. You can contact us at admin@fixifydata.xyz.

What we collect

  • Contact form. Your name, work email, company, website, the areas you picked, where you are today, your message, and your agreement to this policy.
  • Where you came from. With the form we also record the page you landed on, the site that referred you, and campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source), so we know which channels work.
  • Technical details sent with the form. Your browser type and your country (worked out from your IP address by our host). We don’t store your full IP address.
  • Analytics (only if you accept). If you accept analytics cookies, we measure page views, clicks, form steps and how far you scroll, using Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics. If you reject, none of this runs.
  • Your cookie choice. Stored in your browser so we don’t ask every visit.
  • Email. If you email us, we keep the message and your address.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To reply to you and discuss a possible project: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, and steps you ask us to take before a contract.
  • To understand which channels and pages work: your consent (analytics only).
  • To keep the site secure and stop spam: our legitimate interest.

We don’t sell your data. We don’t send newsletters from the contact form.

Who processes it for us

  • Vercel: website hosting and the function that receives the form.
  • Google: stores form submissions in Google Sheets and sends us the notification email; provides Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics if you accept analytics; serves the fonts used on this site, which means your browser contacts Google’s font servers when a page loads.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK and EU. Where that happens, they rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision. [Confirm the transfer mechanism for each provider.]

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time (use “Cookie settings” in the footer).
